# INTERLUDE — Minimalist Pure Black Aesthetic

## Core Direction
- **Ground:** True Pitch Black (`#000000`), raw and unadorned.
- **Surface Contrast:** `#0a0a0a` / `#121212` with subtle `#1a1a1a` borders.
- **Ink & Signal:** Pure crisp white (`#ffffff`), restrained muted tones (`#737373`, `#404040`), green/red status indicators.
- **Typography:** Display `Syne` (clean, bold, confident), UI `Plus Jakarta Sans`, readouts `JetBrains Mono`.
- **Zero Fluff:** Purged all fake tech slogans, marketing filler, telemetry banners, checkmark pills, and decorative bloat. Focused strictly on what matters: room code, song search, queue, and lyrics.
